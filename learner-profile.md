## Known weak spots / recurring errors
- Doubles consonant in "Guten" → writes "Gutten"
- Lowercase "ihnen" — needs to remember formal Sie/Ihnen always capitalized
- Word order with "auch" — was recurring (L1–L6), but drilled in review session and got 4/4 correct — may be solidifying
- "Es geht's" (wrong) vs "Es geht" (correct) in answers
- Adjective endings: -e vs -es (e.g. *eine schöne Stadt* not *eine schönes Stadt*)
- Forgets to capitalize nouns mid-sentence (Stadt, Nachname, Lieblingsstadt)
- Mixes English/German words: "English" instead of "Englisch", "is" instead of "ist" (persistent across L3–L5)
- Capitalizes "Ich" mid-sentence (recurring across multiple lessons — needs drilling)
- Masculine nouns after haben: uses "ein/kein" instead of "einen/keinen" (first seen L5) — drilled in review session, showed clear improvement
- *kein/keine* for plurals still slipping (wrote "kein Kinder" → should be "keine Kinder")
- Irregular plurals: drops umlaut (Kinde → Kinder, Sohne → Söhne) — needs drilling
- leben vs lieben confusion (one letter difference — to live vs to love)
- *möchte* vs *möchten*: applies regular -t ending to er (*möchtet*) — modal verbs don't do this; ich = er = möchte
- English words still sneaking in: "beer" instead of "Bier" (same pattern as "is"/"English" in earlier lessons)
- *einen Kaffee* (masc. acc.) keeps slipping — reverts to *ein Kaffee* even after correction in L7
- Umlaut plurals: forgets umlaut on Supermarkt→Supermärkte, Stadt→Städte
- Verb agreement with collective singular: wrote *wohnen Ihre Familie* → should be *wohnt* (Familie is singular)
- *schön* vs *schon*: wrote *schon* (already) instead of *schön* (nice) in a set phrase

## Notes for next time (updated L12)
- *ist* instead of "is" — still the single most persistent error, recurring in nearly every lesson through L12; needs a dedicated warm-up drill rather than just in-context correction
- Sentence-start capitals (*ich*, *sie*, *die*) still dropping — also recurring across nearly every lesson; pair with the *ist* drill above
- Indefinite dative (*eine→einer*) introduced in L12 — one slip in-session, but used correctly unprompted in homework (*in einer Wohnung*) — solidifying fast
- *bei* vs *in*: uses "in" for companies — correct to *bei* (bei Google, bei Smart Steel Technologies)
- Professions with sein: drop the article (*ich bin Arzt*, not *ich bin ein Arzt*) — first introduced L5, not yet solid
- Modal bracket rule: places *nicht* after infinitive — must be before it (*nicht kommen*); also broke bracket in free writing L11 (*kann fahren Fahrrad* → *kann Fahrrad fahren*)
- Gender agreement with pronouns: used *Es* for *die Katze* → must be *Sie*
- New-word spelling slips are noise, not a pattern (Bett→Bet, Stuhl→Schtul in L12) — one correction each was enough

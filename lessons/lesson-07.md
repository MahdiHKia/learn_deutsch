## Lesson goal (can-do)
By the end of this lesson I can:
- order food and drink politely
- say what I'd like
- use einen/eine/ein when ordering

## Vocabulary themes
Essen & Trinken: der Kaffee, der Tee, das Wasser, das Bier, das Brot, der Kuchen, die Suppe, die Pizza; Ich möchte…, Ich nehme…, Was möchten Sie?, die Speisekarte, die Rechnung, zahlen bitte

## Grammar focus
- **ich möchte** (would like) + noun
- **Accusative — first taste**: der→den, ein→einen (masculine changes!), die/das stay the same
- Polite ordering phrases

## Suggested session flow
1. Café vocabulary + the menu
2. Teach 'ich möchte' as the polite ordering tool
3. Introduce accusative gently: only masculine visibly changes (einen Kaffee)
4. Role-play: ordering in a café, then asking for the bill
5. Homework: order a 3-item meal in writing

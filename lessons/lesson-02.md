## Lesson goal (can-do)
By the end of this lesson I can:
- say where I come from and where I live
- say which languages I speak
- ask others these same questions

## Vocabulary themes
kommen aus, wohnen in, sprechen, Land/Länder (Deutschland, Österreich, die Schweiz, …), Stadt, die Sprache (Deutsch, Englisch, …), Woher? Wo? Was?

## Grammar focus
- **W-questions**: Wie? Woher? Wo? Was? Wer? + word order (verb in position 2)
- Present tense of **kommen, wohnen, sprechen** (ich/du/Sie + er/sie)
- Prepositions **aus** (origin) and **in** (location) — basic use

## Suggested session flow
1. Review intro from L1 briefly (no full recap, just a warmup)
2. Teach kommen aus / wohnen in with a map of countries
3. Teach sprechen + languages
4. Drill W-questions and answering them
5. Homework: write 4 sentences about myself (name, origin, residence, language)

# Lesson 09 — The Accusative Case & Negation

> One lesson = one session. Read this file at the start; fill in the
> **Post-session summary** at the end. (See `CLAUDE.md` for the full ritual.)

## Lesson goal (can-do)
By the end of this lesson I can:
- use the accusative case confidently
- negate sentences with nicht and kein
- say what I do and don't have/want

## Vocabulary themes
review food/objects vocab, nicht, kein/keine/keinen, doch

## Grammar focus
- **Accusative case fully**: the direct object; article changes (der→den, ein→einen; die/das/eine/ein unchanged)
- **kein** (no/not a) vs. **nicht** (not) — when to use which
- Position of nicht in a sentence

## Suggested session flow
1. Consolidate accusative from L7 into the full picture with a clear table
2. Drill direct objects with several verbs (haben, kaufen, brauchen, möchten)
3. Teach kein vs. nicht with contrasting examples
4. Practice negating statements
5. Homework: 6 sentences, half negated with kein, half with nicht

---

## Post-session summary
- Accusative table drilled across all 3 genders — masc. change (einen/keinen) now solid
- kein vs nicht distinction understood well; spotted definite vs indefinite negation difference unprompted
- nicht position: goes before adjectives (*nicht teuer*), after definite noun (*den Mantel nicht*)
- Recurring error: *er möchtet* → modal verbs don't take -t for er; ich = er = möchte
- Gender mistake: *das Tasche* → *die Tasche* (feminine)
- *ich* capitalized mid-sentence still recurring — most persistent error across all lessons
- Bonus: introduced doch and modal verbs concept (preview of L10)

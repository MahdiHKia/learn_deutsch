# Lesson 03 — Numbers, Age & Phone Numbers

> One lesson = one session. Read this file at the start; fill in the
> **Post-session summary** at the end. (See `CLAUDE.md` for the full ritual.)

## Lesson goal (can-do)
By the end of this lesson I can:
- count and use numbers 0–100
- say and ask someone's age
- give and understand a phone number
- understand simple prices

## Vocabulary themes
die Zahlen 0–100, Wie alt bist du? / Ich bin … Jahre alt, die Telefonnummer, der Euro, der Cent, Wie viel? / Was kostet das?

## Grammar focus
- **Numbers 0–100** — note the 'flip' (einundzwanzig = one-and-twenty)
- **wie alt** + sein for age
- Reading numbers aloud for phone numbers and prices

## Suggested session flow
1. Build numbers 0–20, then tens, then the 21+ flip pattern (this trips people up)
2. Drill listening: I say a number, you write it; you say one, I confirm
3. Age questions and answers
4. Phone numbers and simple prices
5. Homework: count to 100, say my age and phone number out loud

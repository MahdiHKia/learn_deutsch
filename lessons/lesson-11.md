# Lesson 11 — Time & Daily Routine

> One lesson = one session. Read this file at the start; fill in the
> **Post-session summary** at the end. (See `CLAUDE.md` for the full ritual.)

## Lesson goal (can-do)
By the end of this lesson I can:
- tell and ask the time
- name days of the week and parts of the day
- describe my daily routine with separable verbs

## Vocabulary themes
die Uhrzeit, Wie spät ist es?, um … Uhr, halb/Viertel, die Tage (Montag–Sonntag), morgens/mittags/abends; separable verbs: aufstehen, anfangen, einkaufen, fernsehen, aufräumen

## Grammar focus
- **Telling time** — official (13:30) and everyday (halb zwei, Viertel nach)
- **Separable verbs**: prefix splits off and goes to the end (Ich stehe um 7 Uhr auf)

## Suggested session flow
1. Teach clock time, both styles; drill 'Wie spät ist es?'
2. Days of the week + parts of day
3. Introduce separable verbs and the splitting rule
4. Have me describe a typical day
5. Homework: write my daily routine in 5 sentences

---

## Post-session summary
- Clock time drilled well — halb/Viertel nach/vor all correct; halb X = half to X clicked immediately
- Days of week and parts of day (morgens/abends etc.) learned quickly with no errors
- Separable verbs: split rule understood; main struggles were vowel changes (er sieht/fängt) and conjugating ich (stehe not stehen)
- Daily routine paragraph good but recurring issues: "ist" written as "is" (3×), modal bracket broken (kann fahren Fahrrad), verb-second after time phrase (Am Samstag ich kaufe)
- telc A1 mock exam: 31/40 (77%) — above pass mark; weak spots: möchte vs möchten, kein/keine gender, modal bracket in free writing

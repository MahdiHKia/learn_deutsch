## Lesson goal (can-do)
By the end of this lesson I can:
- talk about past events in simple terms
- form the Perfekt with haben and sein
- use common past participles

## Vocabulary themes
review of daily-life verbs; gestern, letzte Woche, am Wochenende; participles: gemacht, gespielt, gekauft, gegessen, getrunken, gegangen, gefahren, gewesen

## Grammar focus
- **Perfekt tense**: haben/sein (position 2) + **past participle at the end**
- Forming participles: regular ge-…-t (gemacht) vs. common irregulars (gegessen, gegangen)
- When to use **sein** as helper (movement/change: gegangen, gefahren)

## Suggested session flow
1. Explain the Perfekt structure and the participle-at-the-end rule
2. Regular participle formation, then a set of high-frequency irregulars
3. The haben vs. sein helper choice
4. Tell me to describe what I did yesterday
5. Homework: 5 sentences about my weekend in the Perfekt; this completes A1!

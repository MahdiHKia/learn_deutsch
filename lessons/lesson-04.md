> **Post-session summary** at the end. (See `CLAUDE.md` for the full ritual.)

## Lesson goal (can-do)
By the end of this lesson I can:
- use der/die/das with common nouns
- conjugate regular verbs in the present tense
- form simple statements

## Vocabulary themes
der/die/das, common everyday nouns, regular verbs: machen, lernen, arbeiten, spielen, trinken, kommen

## Grammar focus
- **Noun gender** der/die/das — why every noun has a gender, tips for guessing, learn nouns *with* their article
- **Regular present-tense conjugation**: -e, -st, -t, -en, -t, -en (full paradigm)
- Verb in **position 2** in statements

## Suggested session flow
1. Explain grammatical gender and the habit of learning 'der Tisch' not just 'Tisch'
2. Full present-tense endings with 2–3 verbs in a table
3. Drill conjugation with different subjects
4. Build simple sentences (Subject + verb + rest)
5. Homework: conjugate 3 verbs, learn 8 nouns with articles

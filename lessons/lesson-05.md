# Lesson 05 — sein & haben; Asking Questions

> One lesson = one session. Read this file at the start; fill in the
> **Post-session summary** at the end. (See `CLAUDE.md` for the full ritual.)

## Lesson goal (can-do)
By the end of this lesson I can:
- use haben fully in the present
- ask and answer yes/no questions
- describe what I have

## Vocabulary themes
haben (full), sein (review), yes/no question vocab, Hast du…? Haben Sie…?, schon/noch, ein Auto, eine Katze, ein Kind

## Grammar focus
- **haben** full conjugation (habe, hast, hat, haben, habt, haben)
- **Yes/No questions**: verb goes to position 1 (Hast du Zeit?)
- Short answers: Ja / Nein, contrast with W-questions from L2

## Suggested session flow
1. Teach haben fully; contrast with sein from L1
2. Show the word-order switch for yes/no questions
3. Drill: turn statements into yes/no questions and answer them
4. Mini-dialogue using haben (Hast du…?)
5. Homework: 5 yes/no questions + answers

---

## Post-session summary
- Drilled haben full conjugation — mastered cold, no errors
- sein vs haben contrast — clear distinction, no confusion
- Yes/no questions (verb to position 1) — structure solid; one error: "Kommest Sie" instead of "Kommen Sie" (formal Sie uses infinitive)
- kein/keine/keinen — grasped quickly; masculine keinen/einen still slipping (writes "kein Hund" instead of "keinen Hund")
- Recurring: capitalizes "Ich" mid-sentence, writes "is" instead of "ist"
- New rule introduced: professions with sein drop the article (ich bin Arzt, not ein Arzt) — not yet solid

## Lesson goal (can-do)
By the end of this lesson I can:
- talk about hobbies and free-time activities
- say what I like / prefer doing
- review and tighten word order

## Vocabulary themes
die Freizeit, das Hobby, Sport machen, lesen, schwimmen, tanzen, Musik hören, Freunde treffen, kochen, reisen; gern, lieber, am liebsten

## Grammar focus
- **gern / lieber / am liebsten** to express liking and preference (Ich spiele gern Fußball)
- **Word-order review**: position-2 verb, time-manner-place, modal bracket — consolidate everything so far

## Suggested session flow
1. Hobby vocabulary
2. Teach gern/lieber for likes and preferences
3. Use this lesson to *review* word order with varied sentences
4. Conversation about what I do in my free time
5. Homework: 5 sentences about hobbies using gern/lieber

## Lesson goal (can-do)
By the end of this lesson I can:
- name places in a town
- ask for and understand simple directions
- give simple instructions with the imperative

## Vocabulary themes
die Stadt: der Bahnhof, die Bank, die Post, der Supermarkt, das Restaurant, die Apotheke, das Krankenhaus; links, rechts, geradeaus, Wo ist…?, Wie komme ich zu…?

## Grammar focus
- Asking directions: **Wo ist…? / Wie komme ich zum/zur…?**
- **Imperative (basic)**: Gehen Sie…, Nehmen Sie…, Geh…, formal & informal commands

## Suggested session flow
1. Places-in-town vocabulary
2. Teach asking for directions
3. Introduce the imperative (formal Sie-form first, then du)
4. Role-play: lost tourist asking the way
5. Homework: ask for and give directions to 2 places

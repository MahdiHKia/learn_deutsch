# Lesson 01 — Greetings & Introducing Yourself

> **Post-session summary** at the end. (See `CLAUDE.md` for the full ritual.)

## Lesson goal (can-do)
By the end of this lesson I can:
- greet people and say goodbye (formal & informal)
- say my name and ask someone's name
- spell my name using the German alphabet
- ask how someone is and answer

## Vocabulary themes
Hallo, Guten Morgen/Tag/Abend, Tschüss, Auf Wiedersehen, ich heiße…, mein Name ist…, Wie heißen Sie? / Wie heißt du?, Wie geht's? / Wie geht es Ihnen?, gut/schlecht/super, ja/nein, danke, bitte

## Grammar focus
- The verb **sein** (to be): ich bin, du bist, Sie sind
- **Formal vs. informal**: du vs. Sie — when to use each
- The **German alphabet** & spelling out loud (buchstabieren)

## Suggested session flow
1. Warm greetings + the formal/informal split with examples
2. Teach ich heiße / mein Name ist; drill 'Wie heißt du?' both directions
3. Walk through the alphabet; have me spell my own name and a few words
4. Mini-dialogue: two people meeting for the first time
5. Homework: be able to introduce myself in 3 sentences

# Lesson 08 — Plurals, Shopping & Prices

> **Post-session summary** at the end. (See `CLAUDE.md` for the full ritual.)

## Lesson goal (can-do)
By the end of this lesson I can:
- form and recognise noun plurals
- talk about quantities
- ask what things cost while shopping

## Vocabulary themes
shopping vocab: der Supermarkt, das Geschäft, kaufen, brauchen; quantities: ein Kilo, ein Liter, eine Flasche, ein Stück; Was kostet…? Was kosten…? teuer/billig/günstig

## Grammar focus
- **Noun plurals** — the main patterns (-e, -er, -(e)n, -s, umlaut) and why plurals must be memorised
- **kosten** singular vs. plural (kostet / kosten)
- Quantity expressions

## Suggested session flow
1. Explain that German plurals are irregular and learned per noun; show the patterns
2. Practice making plurals of known nouns
3. Shopping vocabulary + quantities
4. Role-play at a market asking prices
5. Homework: pluralise 8 nouns, price-asking dialogue

---

## Post-session summary
- Covered all 5 plural patterns with drills; umlaut plurals (Städte, Supermärkte) are the weak spot
- *kostet* vs *kosten* (singular/plural) nailed immediately — 3/3 on first drill
- Quantity words (ein/eine/einen) explained and drilled — grasped the logic quickly, got 4/4
- Market role-play went well; main recurring error: *einen Kaffee* (masculine accusative) still slipping
- Combined L1–L8 exercise revealed: verb agreement with singular collectives (*wohnt* not *wohnen* for Familie), *schön* vs *schon*, and sentence chaining without a subject
- Overall strong session — concepts clear, production improving

## Lesson goal (can-do)
By the end of this lesson I can:
- talk about my family
- use mein/dein/sein/ihr
- say who belongs to whom

## Vocabulary themes
die Familie: Mutter, Vater, Eltern, Bruder, Schwester, Sohn, Tochter, Kind, Mann, Frau, Oma, Opa; mein/dein/sein/ihr/unser

## Grammar focus
- **Possessive articles**: mein/dein/sein/ihr — and that they agree with the noun's gender (mein Vater, meine Mutter)
- Light note that endings shift by gender/number

## Suggested session flow
1. Family vocabulary with articles
2. Possessives mein/dein, then sein/ihr
3. The mein/meine gender agreement (practice both)
4. Have me describe my own family
5. Homework: write 4 sentences about my family

## Lesson goal (can-do)
By the end of this lesson I can:
- describe where I live and the rooms
- name furniture
- say where things are using prepositions

## Vocabulary themes
die Wohnung, das Haus, das Zimmer, die Küche, das Bad, das Schlafzimmer, das Wohnzimmer; Möbel: der Tisch, der Stuhl, das Bett, das Sofa, der Schrank; in/auf/unter/neben

## Grammar focus
- **Prepositions of place** in/auf/unter/neben/vor/hinter (location)
- **Dative — gentle intro**: location answers 'wo?' and uses dative (in der Küche, auf dem Tisch) — keep it light at A1

## Suggested session flow
1. Rooms and furniture vocabulary with articles
2. Teach location prepositions answering 'wo?'
3. Introduce dative only as needed for these phrases (dem/der), no deep theory
4. Describe my own home
5. Homework: describe one room and where 4 things are

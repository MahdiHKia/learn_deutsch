# Course Structure — German A1

This is the full curriculum. It is **static** — written once, not updated each
session. It maps roughly to the Goethe-Zertifikat A1 / CEFR A1 scope.

**Goal of the course:** by the end I can introduce myself, handle simple everyday
situations (shopping, café, asking for things, telling time, talking about family
and daily routine), ask and answer simple questions, and talk about the past in
basic terms.

There are **16 lessons**. Each has its own file in `lessons/`. Do one lesson per
session. See `progress.md` for where I currently am.

| #  | Lesson file        | Topic                                   | Grammar focus |
|----|--------------------|-----------------------------------------|---------------|
| 1  | `lesson-01.md`     | Greetings & introducing yourself        | sein (ich/du/Sie), alphabet, formal vs. informal |
| 2  | `lesson-02.md`     | Personal info & countries               | W-questions, kommen/wohnen/sprechen, countries & languages |
| 3  | `lesson-03.md`     | Numbers, age & phone numbers            | numbers 0–100, "wie alt", prices |
| 4  | `lesson-04.md`     | Nouns, gender & the present tense       | der/die/das, regular verb conjugation |
| 5  | `lesson-05.md`     | sein & haben; asking questions          | haben fully, yes/no questions, word order |
| 6  | `lesson-06.md`     | Family & possessives                    | mein/dein/sein/ihr, family vocab |
| 7  | `lesson-07.md`     | Food, drink & the café                  | "ich möchte", accusative intro (einen/eine/ein) |
| 8  | `lesson-08.md`     | Plurals, shopping & prices              | noun plurals, quantities, "Was kostet...?" |
| 9  | `lesson-09.md`     | The accusative case & negation          | full accusative, kein vs. nicht |
| 10 | `lesson-10.md`     | Modal verbs                             | können / müssen / möchten, verb at the end |
| 11 | `lesson-11.md`     | Time & daily routine                    | telling time, days, separable verbs |
| 12 | `lesson-12.md`     | Home & living                           | rooms/furniture, prepositions of place, dative intro |
| 13 | `lesson-13.md`     | Free time & hobbies                     | gern/lieber, word-order review |
| 14 | `lesson-14.md`     | In town: directions & places            | places in town, basic imperative |
| 15 | `lesson-15.md`     | Health, body & the doctor               | body parts, "Was fehlt Ihnen?", weh tun |
| 16 | `lesson-16.md`     | Talking about the past (Perfekt)        | Perfekt with haben/sein, past participles |

After Lesson 16 I should be roughly at the level needed for a Start Deutsch 1 /
A1 exam. We can then add review sessions as needed.
